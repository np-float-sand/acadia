## What Was Attempted This Session

### Goal
Implement per-ticker zone GSI for PJM to break the beta-clustering problem: all 5 PJM tickers sharing a single system-hub GSI → similar scores → no cross-sectional dispersion → broken rankings.

### Hypothesis
If each PJM ticker uses its home-zone LMP (e.g. EXC gets PECO/BGE/PEPCO/COMED zone prices instead of Western Hub), the stress signal should vary more across tickers, improving IC and Sharpe.

### Result
Hypothesis rejected. Zone GSI made IC worse (0.146 → 0.077 at 21d). Root cause: PJM tickers are **rate-regulated T&D utilities**, not merchant generators. Their revenues are determined by rate cases, not zone LMPs. The ERCOT approach works because VST/NRG are merchant generators directly exposed to hub prices. PJM T&D companies don't have that pass-through.

## PJM Zone GSI — Why It Failed

**High-level:** The zone GSI produced different signals across PJM tickers (FE: 775 stress days, D: 691, PEG: 706 vs system PJM: 715) but IC fell from 0.146 → 0.077. Cross-sectional variation increased but in directions that don't predict equity returns.

**Root cause — business model mismatch:**
- ERCOT tickers (VST, NRG) are **merchant generators**. Their earnings are directly tied to real-time and day-ahead hub prices. A LMP spike in HB_NORTH immediately flows through to margin. GSI → equity return linkage is direct and mechanistic.
- PJM tickers (AEP, EXC, PPL, FE, D, PEG) are **rate-regulated T&D utilities**. Revenues are set by rate cases filed with state regulators (Virginia SCC, Illinois ICC, PA PUC, NJ BPU, etc.). LMP congestion in ATSI or PSEG zones does NOT directly change EXC's or FE's quarterly earnings. The link is indirect and noisy: congestion might eventually show up in rate case testimonies or capex justifications, but not in quarterly earnings in any predictable way.

## Stress Betas — What We Know

VST and NRG consistently have strongly negative stress betas (they UNDERPERFORM relative to sector on stress days — counterintuitive for generators, suggests the "excess return vs sector" construction penalizes them because the sector average is dragged up by the few stocks that benefit). EXC and PCG are also consistently short.

The "generator paradox": on stress days, the sector average return goes up (generators like VST/NRG do well in absolute terms), but the EXCESS return (stock minus sector) for those generators may be negative because their absolute return is below the sector spike. This is a construction artifact worth reviewing.

## Open Questions / Brainstorm Topics (Next Session)

The central question: **what signal actually differentiates PJM T&D utilities if not LMP zone stress?**

**Option A — Remove PJM from universe entirely**
- Pros: recovers 0.272–0.315 Sharpe cleanly, strategy remains valid
- Cons: 6 large-cap utilities out of scope, significant missed coverage

**Option B — Interest Coverage Ratio (ICR)**  
- Already built: `equity_prices.fetch_icr()`, `resilience_score.build_factor()` has ICR wired (15% weight, off by default), `USE_ICR = False` in config, `--icr / --no-icr` CLI flag
- Hypothesis: low ICR = leveraged utility = vulnerable to rate stress events (rate hikes hurt refinancing cost)
- Limitation: yfinance only returns ~4–12 quarters of history per ticker, weak in pre-2022 window
- Better source: EDGAR XBRL filings would give 10+ years
- Action: run `--icr` and check if PJM IC improves

**Option C — Regulatory rate case calendar**
- Hypothesis: utilities that haven't had a rate case recently are "under-earning" (test period data is stale). Utilities with pending rate cases have more earnings uncertainty.
- Data: FERC/state PUC dockets are public but unstructured
- Complexity: medium-high, requires new data source

**Option D — Storm/outage reliability signal (NERC/EIA)**
- Hypothesis: utilities with more frequent SAIDI/SAIFI outage events (EIA-861 annual survey) are more exposed to capex burden from storm hardening
- Data: EIA-861 is annual, public, already in structured form
- Relevance to PJM: Northeast corridor (EXC, FE, PEG) has very different storm profiles than Appalachian/Midwest (AEP, PPL, D)
- Action: pull EIA-861 SAIDI by utility and backtest as a factor component

**Option E — Exclude PJM tickers from the GSI factor, keep them in universe on different factor**
- Use GSI beta for ERCOT/MISO/CAISO/SPP tickers, use ICR or capex/rate-base growth for PJM tickers
- Blends two different signal types in the same portfolio
- Architecture: `build_factor()` would need ISO-conditional weighting

**Option F — RT/DA spread as 5th GSI sub-signal (PJM-specific)**  
- Still in CLAUDE.md future work: real-time vs day-ahead spread signals generators vs T&D
- Infrastructure ~90% ready: needs `ISO_RT_LOCATION_TYPE` config dict and `fetch_lmp_rt()` variant
- May help differentiate EXC (nuclear gen + T&D) from PPL (pure T&D)
- Would not help much for purely regulated utilities (AEP, FE, D, PEG)

**Most promising immediate action:** Run with `--icr` to see if ICR factor improves PJM IC. Low effort (already built), meaningful test. If ICR lifts PJM IC materially, it's worth sourcing EDGAR historical data.
