The long book#

Aspect Rules Source
SCREEN
 Suppliers to target
  • Capital spending is low relative to sales (lightest 60%)
  • The 10-K annual report used data center, substation, switchgear, transmission and distribution, grid modernization or electric utilities in the previous four years
  • Capital spending and sales: SEC EDGAR XBRL filings
  • Wording: SEC EDGAR full-text search of 10-Ks
Profitable
  • Positive earnings, using only figures already filed at the time
  • Not subsidy-dependent: no 10-K mentions of the investment tax credit, production tax credit, advanced manufacturing production credit or net energy metering
  • Earnings: SEC EDGAR XBRL filings
  • Subsidy wording: SEC EDGAR full-text search of 10-Ks
Liquid
  • At least $10m of stock traded on a typical day over the past year
  • A full year of price history
  • Daily prices and volume (Yahoo Finance)
RANK
 Hold the better half
  • Capital intensity: capital spending ÷ sales (lower is better)
  • Revenue acceleration: year-on-year growth in trailing-12-month revenue this quarter, minus last quarter's
  • Score both across the eligible companies, average the two scores, keep the top half
  • SEC EDGAR XBRL filings
WEIGHT
 Equal weight
  • Each name weighted equally
 --
RISK CONTROL
 Volatility target
  • Size the book daily to target 20% annual volatility, never above 1.5×
  • Resize only on moves of more than 20 points of exposure
 --
Stretch de-lever
  • Cut to 80% when the book is over 15% above its 200-day average or far ahead of the market; 60% at extremes
 --
Gold / Treasury sleeve
  • 15% in GLD and IEF, reset monthly
 --

Exact thresholds, timing and the holding buffer are in the rule book.
